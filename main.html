Our Story

Brewed with Love,
Served with Tid

"Kape-tid" is a play on words combining "Kape" (coffee) and "Tid" (time/rest). Founded in 2024, we started with a simple mission: to create a space where people can pause, breathe, and enjoy quality coffee.

Every bean is carefully selected from local Filipino farmers, roasted to perfection, and brewed with precision. We believe that great coffee is not just about taste — it's about the experience, the ambiance, and the connections made over a cup.
